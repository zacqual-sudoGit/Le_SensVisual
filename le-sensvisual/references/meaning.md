# Turn messages into visible expressions

Use when resolving the core's [visual decisions](../SKILL.md#create-as-one-coordinated-task). Keep planning internal unless a consequential choice needs the user.

## Resolve the expression

Identify the subject's setting, people, objects, materials, actions and relationships. Select what appears, where it sits beside the information and what it helps the audience recognise, understand or experience. Atmosphere and visual unity are useful benefits alongside literal explanation.

Make these decisions for valuable supporting content as well as the opening. Give them room within a coherent image, colour and type language, with calm reading fields. A character can identify a group; an action needs a visible action when that is the message. Choose one relevant [reference](native-calibration.md) and transfer its functions into this subject.

## Worked decisions: supporting worlds and relationships

| Reference and message | Visible expression and placement | Audience benefit |
|---|---|---|
| [Gardens](../examples/gardens/preview.jpg): different visitors have different needs | A shared visitor-planning scene is accompanied by separate family/stroller, traveller/map, older-visitor/bench and staff/counter scenes within their audience cards. Matching symbols and colour roles link each scene to its copy. | Identifies each audience in the same place without losing the distinctions. |
| Gardens: planning has several difficulties | A map, weather objects, time/walking objects and speech bubbles sit beneath the main scene with short labels. | Makes different planning concerns recognisable at a glance. |
| [Haze](../examples/haze.jpg): conditions have causes and consequences | A hazy skyline and smoky landscapes establish conditions around quiet copy. A map joins fire sources, smoke-carrying wind arrows and the affected city; people and a phone connect explanation with action. | Shows where smoke originates, how it travels and whom it affects within the same world. |
| [LoopLunch](../examples/looplunch.jpg): participation and operations form a loop | Coworkers and pickup/return shelves establish place. Ordering, collection and return receive distinct action scenes beside deadlines; a separate preparation/delivery/wash loop explains the service around them. | Shows who does what, where and when, and how returned containers become ready again. |

For a new subject, resolve its own local scenes, actions and objects. Carry their subjects, relationships, placement and shared treatment into the artwork brief or assembly; a rich header alone cannot supply these supporting benefits. Scene detail can fade towards text while remaining recognisable. Select treatments for their benefit rather than copying cards, opacity, palette or asset counts.

For teaching, make the relevant changing states or mechanism visible and link them to precise labels, formulas or code. Depicted counts and connections must match the explanation. These references calibrate visual decisions; their historical facts, illustrative interfaces and unmeasured claims are not reusable evidence.

## Worked decisions: a service experience

Inspect [NLB](../examples/nlb-proposal.jpg). Its library setting joins the opening and smaller service scenes; the following decisions give those scenes different jobs.

| Message | Visible expression and placement | Audience benefit |
|---|---|---|
| Ask in everyday language | A reader enters a short request on a tablet beside the first step's explanation. | Makes starting the service tangible. |
| Discover and refine choices | Distinct book covers, a shortlist and exploration controls occupy the next two stages. | Shows what readers can consider and change. |
| Reach human help | A librarian receives the reader at the end of the connected sequence. | Makes the receiving person and handoff visible. |

Compose these as one experience. Let the setting and materials continue across regions while protecting requests, choices and explanations. For a complete image, brief the reader, actual request, distinct choices, receiving librarian and their relationships together. For assembly, obtain those same scenes/objects and combine them with the readable information. Keep the useful choices and human exchange visible in either method.

During review, identify the visible benefit in each selected region and reconsider useful opportunities the plan omitted. Use the [eleven TYY lenses](tyy-anchor.md) to judge the resulting world, meaning, relationships, composition and harmony.
