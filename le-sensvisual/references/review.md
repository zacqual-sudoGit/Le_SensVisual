# Evaluate TYY achievement with visible evidence

Judge the actual artifact against the original audience need and TYY ambition,
then its production plan. Establish **what intended meaning is visible, where
it is visible, and which important benefit is still missing**. Inspect the world,
atmosphere, actions and supporting expressions, including opportunities omitted
from the plan. A coordinator can detect declared omissions; it cannot discover
opportunities never declared. File validity alone establishes neither visual
achievement nor completion.

## First: visual communication

Use the relevant dimensions below. For each, identify a visible decision, the benefit it supports, and an important missed opportunity. Qualitative judgments such as **strong, partial, absent** need that evidence. Mark a dimension inapplicable with a communication reason when appropriate. Do not manufacture a percentage or hide a weak dimension inside an aggregate score.

| Dimension | What to look for |
|---|---|
| Topic world and atmosphere | Setting, people, materials, objects and tone make this subject recognisable and help the audience enter its context. A topic label and palette alone are weak evidence. |
| Integrated imagery and information | Illustrations, photos, diagrams or evidence reinforce the messages beside them; composition makes the connection apparent. |
| Meaningful relationships | Sequence, handoff, cycle, comparison, grouping or spatial connections express the intended relationship. |
| Content-led composition | Different communication jobs receive appropriate arrangements, with enough identity to form one work. Repetition serves a real navigation or comparison purpose. |
| Calm reading fields and hierarchy | Richness and blank space work together; attention moves through the message and essential information is comfortable to read. |
| Supporting language and harmony | Illustrated objects/icons distinguish content; colour supports grouping or meaning; typography and assets form a coherent system. |
| Evidence and useful closure | Relevant claims connect to available proof; the audience receives the reference, takeaway or next action the job needs. |

Credit real strengths specifically. A visually strong composition remains a design achievement when a separate content defect prevents its use. Conversely, a readable text-card output that bypasses strong topic-specific opportunities fails the design goal. Call that failure plainly and identify the missing expression. Successful files and neat spacing are not evidence of TYY achievement.

Atmosphere, identity, recognition and unity deserve credit alongside literal
explanation. A figure may identify an audience; require an action when that
action is the message. Describe an omission by the audience benefit lost,
without an asset quota or a requirement to apply every lens.

State whether the central goal is achieved, has a material gap, or cannot be
visually verified. A partial rating includes its remaining audience cost;
absence of an "absent" rating does not establish overall success. Non-applicability
needs a communication reason. A review record does not establish that someone
saw or understood the work. Working context suffices; no file schema is required.

## Locate the production gap

Trace a consequential omission to its first observable stage:

- **Recognition:** the subject's useful visual substance was never identified.
- **Selection/composition:** it was named, but no concrete visible treatment or placement was chosen.
- **Tool briefing:** the chosen treatment never reached the responsible tool.
- **Generation/construction:** the requested expression was not returned or built.
- **Assembly:** returned meaning was lost through placement, crop or overlays.
- **Final review:** an observable mismatch remained in the delivered artifact.

Restore missing meaning before cosmetic polish. Keep local-edit reviews within
the affected scope. Trace gaps using actual requests, objects and renders;
separate observations from causal hypotheses when evidence is unavailable. A changed
result alone does not prove an instruction caused it.

## Separately: correctness and production

- **Content:** compare claims, counts, identities and depicted mechanisms with the brief and reliable sources. Plausible imagery can invent facts or connections.
- **Usability:** inspect final viewing-size text, reading order, contrast, collisions, crop and subject visibility. Check consequential action instructions and destinations where supported.
- **Required method/source:** when specified, check that the actual source and assets produced the delivered render. A declared method or output extension is not proof. If that path cannot be inspected, mark it unverified.
- **Requested operations:** inspect actual native text/data/objects for promised edits. State precisely what is editable. Raster previews cannot prove native editing.
- **Delivery:** verify actual formats, page counts, proportions and accessible files/IDs. Inspect each delivered format; an earlier render does not establish a changed export.

Distinguish receiver acceptance, visible integration and TYY benefit. Check the
required parts in the actual delivered canvas for placeholders, broken assets,
blank/clipped regions and loss of meaningful subject detail. Source/DOM references
or embedded bytes establish limited structural facts; they do not prove visible
placement. If the host cannot show the final result, keep visual verification
unconfirmed. Unused generated variants need not appear in the artifact.

For short work inspect every unit. For larger work cover all units for content and obvious failures, with detailed review targeted to risk and distinctive content. Disclose unchecked scope. A consequential unresolved error blocks completion, while the design assessment still records what succeeded.

## Evaluation integrity

Preserve the first delivered result and genuine failures. Distinguish producer-owned corrections during its original attempt from later coaching or evaluator edits. Improvements to an example do not train future users' models; learnings must be implemented in the skill and evaluated on a fresh task.

Separate native skill activation, uploaded guidance and pasted prompts. Record the version, prompt, app/mode, available tools and assistance. Confirm guidance access where observable; acknowledgement alone does not prove application. Different chosen techniques can be described for audience benefit, but differences between image generation and HTML/native construction confound attribution to the skill. A controlled skill-effect comparison needs the same task, host/model/tools, output and construction conditions, with guidance as the intended difference; disclose remaining uncertainty. A restricted construction comparison evaluates that capability. Evaluate TYY achievement, not only tool accuracy. One sample, package validation or self-review does not establish reliability or independent benefit. Run development evaluations only within the owner's approved scope.
