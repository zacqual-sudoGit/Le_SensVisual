# Operate

## Directions

Users must recognise the task, data and controls. Preserve familiar navigation,
actual states, accessibility and efficient repeated use. Build a coherent world
through appropriate imagery, type, palette, density and meaningful relationships.
The subject may organise a useful explanation or data relationship; do not force
it into a fixed number of signature devices or let ornamental costume hide work.

## Comps

Depict the actual product and real/plausible labelled example data, with controls
that could be built and used. Keep status semantics stable. Choose composition
for work areas and reading order. When alternatives are useful, vary a material
structural decision within the identity; don't add clutter just to look different.
