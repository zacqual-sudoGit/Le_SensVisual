# Persuade and Experience

## Directions

For a persuasive surface, make the offer intelligible and desirable, show why it
matters to this audience and give an honest next action. Where a working service
is requested, its real search, booking or purchase interaction must work rather
than be painted into an image. An explanatory poster has a different job; it can
remain an image. For an experience-led surface, let the subject/work itself lead.

The subject world can structure the composition through setting, objects,
material, type, colour and meaningful visual relationships. It need not be a
literal physical object or a theatrical interface. A book, facade or workbench
can carry content when it helps; a calm reading field can carry it too. Avoid
shrinking the subject into a token accent on a generic layout, but do not make
decoration compete with useful reading and action.

When the world is represented as a physical object, keep its construction and
depicted actions plausible unless deliberate stylization serves the brief. Do
not add impossible folds/extra copies merely to accommodate a poorly planned
layout. For a working page retain semantic navigation, accessible contrast,
legible copy, recognizable controls and useful responsive behavior.

## Comps

If previews are requested, explore the uncertainty worth resolving rather than
forcing each successive variant to become louder. Clarity, atmosphere and
specificity can improve together. Use visualize.md for the conditional preview
method; no fixed count or compulsory image-first round is imposed here.
