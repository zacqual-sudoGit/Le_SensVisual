# Read

## Directions

Readers need a clear explanation, wayfinding and usable reference detail.
Protect calm reading fields and comfortable typography. Let the subject world
enter the explanation itself through objects, scenes, mechanisms, annotated
examples and materials where they aid recognition, meaning or experience.
Do not confine it to a decorative masthead or turn every idea into a text card.
Keep dense code/formulas usable, with copyable companions when appropriate.

## Comps

Choose the format and construction for the reader's task. If visual alternatives
are needed, vary content-led composition and emphasis, not only surface styling.
Use real content, true relationships and enough detail to demonstrate the idea.
Image, native and mixed construction are valid. Inspect the actual explanation
for topic identity, selected opportunities, reading comfort and correctness.
